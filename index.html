02 / The Vision

HSE 5.0™

From traditional safety management toward an integrated, predictive and human-centric safety paradigm.

From Reactive Safety to Predictive, Human-Centric, Connected and Autonomous Safety.

HSE 5.0 is a strategic vision and developing framework that brings people, organizational governance, risk intelligence, digital capabilities and resilience into a connected approach to HSE.

The purpose is not to replace human expertise with technology, but to strengthen human decision-making through better information, earlier risk recognition and responsible innovation.

01 Reactive — Learning from incidents and losses
02 Preventive — Hazard identification and risk control
03 Digital — Connected information and systems
04 Predictive — Weak signals and risk intelligence
05 HSE 5.0 — Human-centric, connected and resilient safety
