What is HSE 5.0?

HSE 5.0 is a forward-looking vision and management framework designed to evolve Health, Safety and Environment beyond traditional reactive approaches. It integrates human intelligence, artificial intelligence, predictive analytics, digital connectivity, organizational governance and resilience into a unified safety management philosophy.
