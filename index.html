Majid Roshan works across industrial health, safety and environment, with professional experience connected to major steelmaking, direct-reduction and industrial development activities.

His professional interests focus on strengthening HSE governance, critical risk control, workforce participation and the responsible use of digital technologies and artificial intelligence in safety management.

The HSE 5.0 vision brings these elements into a coherent direction: predictive capability supported by human judgement, transparent governance and resilient operational systems.

02 / The Vision

HSE 5.0™

A strategic vision for the next evolution of health, safety and environmental management.

A transition toward predictive, human-centric, connected and autonomous safety — with people and responsible governance at the centre.

HSE 5.0 is presented as an integrated management vision and framework. It is not a claim that a finished commercial software platform is currently operational.
