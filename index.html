“The future of safety is not simply more technology. It is better decisions, stronger barriers and people empowered by reliable intelligence.” MAJID ROSHAN · HSE 5.0 VISION

Majid Roshan is a senior HSE professional, strategic consultant and author focused on the evolution of industrial health, safety and environmental management. His professional experience includes involvement across the construction, commissioning and operation lifecycle of major steelmaking and direct-reduction facilities.

His work brings together operational HSE leadership, risk-based thinking, critical-control assurance, emergency preparedness, organizational resilience and the responsible application of digital technologies and artificial intelligence.

As the originator and developer of the HSE 5.0 concept and its evolving framework, he advocates a human-centric approach in which predictive intelligence strengthens — rather than replaces — professional judgement, worker participation and accountable leadership.

Human at the centre Technology supports people, competence and participation.
Risk-informed Decisions connect hazards, barriers, evidence and action.
Resilience-led Organizations anticipate, adapt, respond and learn.
The vision

HSE 5.0 — The next evolution of safety

HSE 5.0 is a developing strategic concept and framework that seeks to integrate human capability, predictive intelligence, connected systems and organizational resilience into a coherent approach to HSE management.

HSE 5.0

Human-centric, connected, adaptive and resilient HSE.

HSE 5.0 is presented here as a concept and framework under development, not as a certified standard, regulator-issued requirement or commercially deployed software product.
