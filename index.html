02 / The Vision

HSE 5.0™

A strategic vision for the next evolution of health, safety and environmental management.

A transition toward predictive, human-centric, connected and autonomous safety — with people and responsible governance at the centre.

HSE 5.0 is an integrated management vision and framework intended to connect human capabilities, organizational governance, risk intelligence and advanced technologies.

Its purpose is to strengthen the ability of organizations to anticipate risk, recognize weak signals, improve decision-making and develop resilient safety systems.

HSE 5.0 is currently represented through its conceptual architecture and specialized documentation, not as a claim of an operational commercial software platform.
